A one- or two-sentence hook that tells the reader what they'll get. This shows up right under the headline and sets the tone.

Panshul Jindal · Aug 24, 2026 · 8 min read

Open with the problem, not the preamble. The first paragraph should make the reader feel like you're describing something they've run into.

    """Fetch the k most relevant chunks for a query."""
    vector = embed(query)
    hits = index.search(vector, top_k=k)

Use a callout for an aside that's worth pulling out of the flow — a tip, a caveat, a "here's what I'd do differently."
