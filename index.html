About

Engineer first, researcher second.

I'm an AI/ML Engineer with 1.5+ years shipping AI systems that actually reach users. My work sits where research meets production: multi-agent architectures, retrieval-augmented generation, and LLM fine-tuning — built to be observable, cost-aware, and maintainable.

At Kwikpic I built photo-delivery infrastructure serving over 10 million users at 98.9% facial-recognition accuracy, and cut AWS spend by 40% through serverless migration. At UIUC's RailTEC I led research on NL-to-SQL interfaces and computer vision for structural diagnostics.

I care about the unglamorous parts — evaluation harnesses, retrieval quality, latency budgets — because that's usually what separates a demo from a product.
