About

Engineer first, researcher second.

I'm an AI engineer who ships agent systems to production. My work sits where research meets production: agent runtimes, multi-agent orchestration, retrieval-augmented generation and LLM fine-tuning — built to be observable, cost-aware and maintainable.

At Microsoft I built the agent runtime other teams build on, and the queue that lets an agent hand off to its future self so long-running work survives restarts. Before that, at Kwikpic I built photo-delivery infrastructure serving over 10 million users at 98.9% facial-recognition accuracy, and at UIUC's RailTEC I led research on NL-to-SQL interfaces and computer vision for structural diagnostics.

I care about the unglamorous parts — evaluation harnesses, retrieval quality, latency budgets — because that's usually what separates a demo from a product. I care as much about the quickstart as the architecture.

Oh, and I built Friday — my personal AI assistant. You can actually message him on WhatsApp. Fair warning though: he's very loyal, he'll only talk to his boss. Try your luck ↗
